A bit about me

Shropshire-based, usually somewhere muddy

I live in Shropshire, and when I’m not writing you’ll find me in the hills – running, riding my bike or trying to take a half-decent photo. I’m also endlessly curious about the places most people drive straight through, which is how a soft spot for Britain’s new towns turned into a project of its own.

I still write like a journalist – find the real story, cut anything that doesn’t earn its place, file on time. A few years inside a global brand taught me the other half: listening to everyone with an opinion, without losing sight of the reader.
